02 — VERIFY

Check a published image.

Validate a signed proof against the exact downloaded image. A trusted public key can link the signature to a known creator.

✓

Proof verification, explained

A passing proof means the files match and the embedded signature is valid. Without a separately trusted public key, anyone can sign their own image.

Resizing, screenshots, social media compression, or editing will break an exact-byte match.

03 — UNDERSTAND

Built for the real world.
Not for impossible promises.

01

Local by default

All image transformations and signing run inside your browser. Photos are never sent to a server.

02

Visible deterrence

An optional ownership notice communicates your wishes. It can be cropped or erased, so it is not a security boundary.

03

Cryptographic record

A SHA-256 digest and an ECDSA P-256 signature let others check an exact file against a known public key.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION

This app cannot stop every AI edit.

Microtexture is an educational, model-free image transformation—not the learned, gradient-optimized perturbation used in PhotoGuard. ImageGuard does not claim to block AI face swaps, deepfakes, diffusion editors, screenshots, or re-uploads. Even research defenses optimized on models do not reliably transfer to every new model.

For real protection, combine careful sharing, platform reporting, trusted provenance records, and evolving research. Read MIT PhotoGuard ↗ and DiffusionGuard ↗.
